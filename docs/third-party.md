## mmdt-tokenizer — Myanmar word segmentation (MIT)

- Source: PyPI `mmdt-tokenizer` 0.1.3 · Copyright (c) 2025 Myanmar Data Tech
- Used for: the word-segmentation post-processor (Phase 4, owner-approved).
- What is used: `Sources/PyawwCore/Resources/lexicons.json` is copied
  verbatim from the package; the Swift port re-implements the package's
  grammar-rule pipeline (see `Sources/PyawwCore/Segmentation/`) with
  documented adaptations (punctuation is preserved; the output is
  de-spaced between Myanmar word characters before segmenting). The
  reference behaviour and its test vectors are generated by
  `scripts/generate_seg_vectors.py`; the port matches all 209 vector pairs
  exactly (`Tests/PyawwCoreTests/SegmentationTests.swift`).
